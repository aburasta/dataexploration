## Why this works (2026 YouTube best practices)

1. **First ~125 chars are a complete, keyword-rich sentence** — that's the snippet YouTube and
   Google display.
2. **Scope stated explicitly** (war diaries, letters, WWI & WWII) so the algorithm classifies the
   channel correctly and recommends every video to the right audience.
3. **Format named** (dramatized, first-person) to set viewer expectations and differentiate.
4. **Keyword variants woven in naturally** (each 1–2x, no stuffing).
5. **Emotional payoff + target audience** stated.
6. **Upload cadence + Subscribe CTA** for reliability and conversion.
7. **Source/business note** builds trust and signals rights-awareness.
